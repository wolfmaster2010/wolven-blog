About the Den

I'm Steven Anderman — knitter, stay-at-home dad, and the hands behind Wolven Fiber Co. here in Lansing, Michigan.

I taught myself to knit at the beginning of 2023, and it took. These days my stitching runs two ways: beanies on the loom — those are the finished pieces you'll find in the shop — and patterns on the needles, like the washcloths on this blog. Every pattern here is one I've worked up myself first, then written down the way I'd want it handed to me: plain, complete, and honest about the rows.

The "Wolven" isn't decoration. I spent my younger years working around kennels and sled dogs, and wolves have been a thread through my life ever since — they're in the name, in the banner up top, and in our Chief Morale Officer, Clyde, the family husky, who supervises production from the couch and inspects every finished piece.

This blog is the workbench side of the shop: free patterns, notes on what's on the needles, and the occasional update from the den. If a pattern here gives you trouble, the comments on each post are the place to ask — I read everything, and questions get answered.
